A Second Life for Film Cameras

A good camera does not have to be new to have a few stories left in it. At Second Exposure, we help vintage cameras find their next photographer. Browse our collection of film cameras, explore different styles, and find something that makes you want to slow down and take a picture. Whether you’re picking up your first roll of film or returning to an old hobby, you’re welcome here. We believe getting started should feel exciting, not intimidating.

Find Your Next Camera

From manual controls to instant prints, there’s more than one way to capture a moment. Get to know three camera styles you might find on our shelves.

SLR Cameras

SLR cameras offer interchangeable lenses and more control over your settings. They are a good choice for learning photography techniques.

Point-and-Shoot Cameras

These compact cameras keep things simple. Bring one along for everyday snapshots, vacations, and time with friends.

Instant Cameras

Instant cameras produce a physical photograph shortly after you take it. They are fun for parties, gifts, and keepsakes.

Your First Roll Starts Here

New to film? You don’t need a shelf full of equipment to get started. A working camera, compatible film, and a little curiosity are enough for your first outing.

  1. Choose a working camera that fits your interest.
  2. Check which type of film your camera needs.
  3. Read the camera manual and learn its controls.
  4. Take pictures and experiment with different subjects.
  5. Have your roll developed or enjoy your instant prints.
